Stripe — Where the Money Lives

Stripe is the payment processor. Every card payment, payout to your bank, and refund happens in the Stripe Dashboard at dashboard.stripe.com.

$
Payments
Dashboard → Payments lists every sale: amount, customer email, and date. Each one links to its details.
↗
Payouts
Stripe automatically deposits your balance to your bank on a rolling schedule (Dashboard → Payouts). Nothing to do — just confirm your bank is connected.
↺
Refunds
Open the payment → Refund. You can refund the full amount or part of it. The customer gets their money back in a few days.
Test mode vs. Live mode. Stripe has a toggle (top of the Dashboard). Live = real money. If a test purchase didn't charge a real card, you were in Test mode. Make sure the site runs on Live keys when you're truly selling.

Orders — Your Record of Every Sale

Beyond Stripe, every paid order is saved in your Sanity Studio at waterdogproductions.com/studio → Orders. This is your quick reference for who bought what.

▦
Each order shows
Customer email, which photos they bought, the amount paid, the date, and a status.
What the statuses mean
Paid they paid, links are active  ·  Fulfilled optional — you marked it handled  ·  Refunded money returned, downloads cut off
Refunding? Do both steps. Refund the payment in Stripe, then open that order in Orders and set its status to Refunded. That second step is what disables their download links so a refunded customer can't keep grabbing the files.

Customer Service — The Common Questions

Most messages fall into a few buckets. Here's the quick fix for each. Your support address is mehlerc12@gmail.com.

"I paid but didn't get an email."
First have them check spam/junk. Confirm the sale in Stripe → Payments (or Orders in Studio). If it's real, the fastest fix is to email them the photos directly: open each photo in Studio, download the original, and send it.
"My download link won't work / I lost it."
Links can expire from their inbox or get mangled. Confirm the order, then send the original files directly from Studio (as above). This always works and needs no link.
"I want a refund."
Refund in Stripe, then mark the order Refunded in Studio so the links stop working. Use your judgment on partial vs. full.
"Which photo is mine? / Can you find my order?"
Search Orders by their email. It lists exactly which photos they bought and what they paid.
"The photo still has a watermark."
They're looking at the gallery preview, not their purchased file. The watermark-free version is behind the Download button on the success page / email.
"Do you do custom shoots?"
Point them to Book a Session for packages, and have them reach out via Contact.

The One Move That Solves Most Issues

Send the originals directly. If anything goes sideways with links or email, you can always open the customer's photos in Studio, download the full-resolution originals, and email them yourself. Confirm they actually paid (Stripe or Orders) first — then deliver. Done.
