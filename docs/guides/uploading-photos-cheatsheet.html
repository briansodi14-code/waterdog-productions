Quick guide for
Chase

Everything you shoot gets added through the Sanity Studio — your private control panel. Open it in any browser at waterdogproductions.com/studio and sign in. The fastest way to add a session is the Bulk Upload tool. Here's the whole flow.

Bulk Upload — Step by Step

Open the Bulk Upload tool
In the Studio's left sidebar, click Bulk Upload (the ↑ upload icon). This is the screen built for adding many photos at once.
Add your images
Drag & drop photos straight onto the dashed box, or click Browse Files and select them. You can pick as many as you want — hold ⌘ Cmd (Mac) to select multiple at once. Only image files are accepted.
Check the list
The box turns green and shows how many images are selected. Wrong one snuck in? Click the × next to it to remove, or Clear All to start over.
Set the Photo Settings
These apply to every photo in this batch, so upload one date & location at a time:
Date Taken
The day you shot the session (defaults to today).
Location
Pick one: HB Pier · Newport · San Clemente · Laguna.
Featured on Homepage
Tick this only for your best shots — featured photos show on the front page.
Hit Upload & wait for the green check
Click Upload X Photos. A progress bar shows “Uploading 3 of 12…”. Don't close the tab until you see the green “Successfully uploaded” message. Done!
One date + one location per batch. Because the settings apply to the whole upload, do each shoot separately. Shot HB Pier in the morning and Newport in the afternoon? That's two uploads.

Good to Know

● Your originals are safe
Full-resolution files are stored privately. The public gallery only ever shows a watermarked version — customers get the clean original only after they pay.
● They appear fast
New photos show up in the live gallery within about a minute of a successful upload. Refresh the gallery page to see them.
● Featured = front page
The Featured on Homepage checkbox is what puts a photo on the homepage. Keep it to your strongest few so the front page stays sharp.
● Phone or computer
The Studio works in any browser. A computer is easiest for big batches, but you can upload from your phone in a pinch.

Fixing or Removing a Photo

Open the Photo list
In the sidebar, click Photo to see every photo, newest grouped by location and date.
Click the one you want to change
Edit its Date, Location, or Featured toggle — changes save automatically.
To delete it
With the photo open, click the ⋮ menu (top right) → Delete. It disappears from the gallery right away.

If Something Goes Wrong

See a red error message? It's almost always the connection. Check your internet, then click Upload again — already-uploaded photos won't be duplicated for that batch. Still stuck? Note what the red message says and send it to Brian.
